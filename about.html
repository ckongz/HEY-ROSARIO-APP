About Hey Rosario!

Our Story, Mission & Vision

Our Story

Hey Rosario! is Barangay Sto. Rosario's official e-governance platform, designed to bridge the gap between local government and citizens through innovative digital solutions. Born from the vision of modernizing barangay services, this platform empowers residents to access government services, report community issues, and stay informed about barangay activities—all from the convenience of their devices.

Our Mission

To transform barangay governance through technology, making government services more accessible, transparent, and responsive to the needs of every resident of Barangay Sto. Rosario.

Our Vision

A digitally empowered community where every citizen actively participates in local governance, where government services are just a click away, and where transparency and accountability are the foundations of public trust.

Platform Purpose

  • Provide 24/7 access to government services
  • Enable efficient citizen reporting and issue resolution
  • Foster transparent communication between government and citizens
  • Preserve and promote local culture and heritage
  • Enhance emergency response capabilities
  • Support local businesses and tourism

Empowering citizens through digital governance and transparent barangay services.
